A · The drop on the é

The accent of Rosée becomes a dewdrop. Simplest and most elegant: the name carries the logo, and it stays readable at small sizes. Works with the Tunisian star pattern behind it.

B · The arch

An arch window (Tunisian doors and mashrabiya) holding an R and a champagne drop. More recognisable as a symbol, good for the phone icon and social avatars. It echoes the arch shape already in the homepage hero.

C · Star and drop

A star in the Tunisian geometric tradition with the dewdrop at its centre. The most local and ornamental of the three, a bit more complex. Spaced capitals read more luxurious, more like a spa.
